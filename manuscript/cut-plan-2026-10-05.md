# Keep / merge / cut plan for the 2026-10-05 additions

All additions from the digest review are wrapped in `\chg{}` (blue in the review PDF): 42 passages, about 1,930
words, 53 new references, plus Fig. 5 (mind map). The paper grew from 47 to 54 pages.

Ranked across all twelve batches, not within each batch. Criteria, in order:
1. **Directness**: does it support a specific claim the paper already makes?
2. **Strength**: venue, scale, number of models, authorship (single-author and pseudonymous preprints rank lower).
3. **Redundancy**: does another kept citation already make the same point?

Passage numbers (#) follow the order of `\chg{}` blocks in `manuscript.tex`.

## One possible addition (norms)

The phenomena coding labels patterns such as "a convention forms" from behavior. Muralidharan, Kwak, and An (2026,
*Behavior is Not Enough*) and the social-norms literature (Bicchieri 2006; Cialdini, Reno, and Kallgren 1990) argue
that convergent behavior is not by itself a norm. A one-sentence caveat in "Separating phenomena from mechanisms,"
citing Bicchieri and Crawford and Ostrom (already cited), would pre-empt a reviewer objection. See the chat summary
for the wider literature.
