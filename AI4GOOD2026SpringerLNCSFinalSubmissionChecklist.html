AI4GOOD@ICML 2026 Springer LNCS – Final Submission Checklist

Springer requirements are listed below. AI4GOOD additionally requires all ORCIDs in the metadata sheet and one ZIP per paper for collection.

1. Final Paper Files

☐ Use the official Springer LNCS proceedings template and follow Springer's Instructions for Authors and Book Authors' Code of Conduct. For LaTeX authors, use the Springer Nature LNCS template on Overleaf

☐ Submit BOTH the final PDF and the complete final source files. For Word, submit the final .docx file (no .docm) and the corresponding PDF. For LaTeX, submit all .tex files, figures, required style/font files, and .bib/.bbl files, together with the compiled PDF. Submit actual file copies, not links.

☐ The final PDF must correspond exactly to the final source files. Do not include older source versions; submit one final source set and one final PDF. Use short file names.

☐ Include the required Disclosure of Interests statement in the paper. Resolve any third-party permissions before submission, and include approved supplementary material if applicable. Alt text may be included if available; otherwise Springer may generate it during production.

☐ Ensure the paper title, author names, author order, and affiliations are final and correct. Mark exactly ONE corresponding author in the paper; the corresponding author's email address is mandatory. Authorship cannot be changed after delivery to Springer.

☐ If needed, provide an abbreviated running head and any instructions for correct representation of author names (e.g., multiple family names or non-Western name order).

2. Author Metadata

☐ Complete the Author Metadata Sheet: [One paper per row] AI4GOOD ICML2026 Proceeding– Author Metadata

☐ For our workshop metadata collection, enter the OpenReview Submission Number (the 1-600 integer shown to the left of your submission), final paper title, Authors' Given / First Name(s) and Family / Last Name(s) in separate columns, affiliations, exactly one corresponding author, corresponding-author email, all author emails, and all ORCIDs. For multiple authors, separate entries with semicolons (;) and keep the same exact publication order across all author-list fields.

3. Licence-to-Publish (LTP)

☐ Use the pre-filled Springer LTP form:

AI4GOOD_2026_LTP_partially_prefilled.docx

☐ The corresponding author on the LTP must be the same person marked in the paper and metadata sheet. The corresponding author must have permission and authority to sign on behalf of all authors. The signature must be handwritten; digital signatures are not accepted.

☐ The top conference/proceedings field must read: Trustworthy AI for Good Workshop (AI4GOOD@ICML 2026), not an LNCS volume number. Authors requiring a US Government / Crown Copyright or other special form should use the appropriate Springer form; in some cases more than one LTP may be required.

☐ If requesting Open Choice / open access, contact the organizers before final submission; Springer requires the appropriate open-access LTP and invoicing information.

4. ZIP Submission

☐ Create exactly ONE ZIP file for each paper, named: OpenReview_<SubmissionNumber>_<FirstAuthorLastName>.zip

☐ The ZIP must contain actual copies of the final PDF, complete final source files, signed LTP, and any applicable supplementary-material and permission files. Do not submit links, loose files, or multiple versions.

☐ Upload the ZIP to: 01_Author_Submissions

5. Proof Stage

☐ The corresponding author must remain reachable for Springer proof checking (normally about 72 hours). Only typesetting / conversion errors can be corrected at proof stage; title, content, values, and authorship cannot be changed. If no reply is received within the given timeframe, Springer may treat this as confirmation that no errors were found.
